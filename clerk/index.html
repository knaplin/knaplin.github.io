How to Use

  1. Hourly Rates: Add hours. Set price and qty for each court hour.
  2. Shuttlecocks: Add shuttlecocks used. Set price, qty, and toggle which hours (H1, H2) they belong to.
  3. Members: Quick-add or manually add members. Toggle their attendance (✓) per hour to calculate their split.
  4. Deposits: Enter prepaid amounts in the Deposit field to deduct from their final total.
  5. Share: Generate a shareable image or OCR summary to send to your group.
